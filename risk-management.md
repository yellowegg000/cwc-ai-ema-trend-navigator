# Risk Management

## Maximum Risk

- Maximum 1% account risk per trade
- Maximum 5% total exposure across all open positions

## Stop Loss

Every trade must include a stop loss based on 1.5 × ATR.

## Take Profit

Target approximately twice the initial risk using a 3 × ATR profit target.

## Markets to Avoid

- Extremely low-volume periods
- Sideways markets
- High-impact economic announcements
- Flash crashes or abnormal volatility

## Disclaimer

No trading strategy guarantees profits. Users should perform their own research and testing before trading with real funds.
